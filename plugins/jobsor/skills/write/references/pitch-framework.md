# The researched pitch (founder or hiring manager, cold)

From the JOBSOR website (`docs/PITCH_FRAMEWORK.md`, owner's rules 4 to 7 Nov 2026). The goal: a stranger, with no meeting and nobody to
name, reads it, can tell the user did their homework, sees one real thing the user has done that fits, and replies.

## Research first (Exa and Firecrawl)
1. The person's own recent posts, talks, articles and podcasts: search `"<full name>" <company>` with Exa; LinkedIn posts through Exa's
   copy (`site:linkedin.com/posts "<full name>"`), never by opening linkedin.com. Keep only items from the last 90 days that are clearly
   theirs (their name with this company, or their own profile link). Never mix people up.
2. Company news from the last 90 days: launches, funding, hiring, new markets.
3. **Pick the opening: newest first.** Among the 3 newest good items, pick the one closest to the job. Note it in the Messages row with its
   date and link. If nothing real is found, open with the job post itself (what the team is building, in the post's words) and say so in
   Notes.
4. Warnings to tell the user before writing: comments or a post say the role is closed; a post says the person left or joined another
   company. Then stop for that person.

## The message, line by line
| # | Line | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greeting | The user's opener; "Hi {first_name}," by default. Never "Dear Hiring Manager" |
| 2 | **Opening: one specific, recent thing of theirs** | Name the thing and quote their words briefly; "your post" for their post. No praise words |
| 3 | **The user's take: one line** | What the user thinks about it. Offer 3 short options; the user picks or writes their own. Every option is a view on the topic only: **no claim about the user** (never "in my experience", "I have seen", "I've worked on", "I built"), no praise, no numbers. In send-for-me mode, use the plainest option |
| 4 | The job | "Writing about the {role} role at {company}: {job link}" |
| 5 | **One story** | The confirmed fact closest to the opening's subject: where, the problem, what the user did, the result. No technical detail (that is in the resume). Recent work first; something over a year old only if nothing newer fits |
| 6 | Proof | A demo or portfolio link from the profile, if there is one |
| 7 | **Ask** | "If this aligns with you, can we have a quick chat?" |
| 8 | Sign-off | The user's own |
| 9 | PS | Only if the user writes one |

Always: under about 150 words; one resume per company (two people at one company may get different openings and stories, but both stories
must be in the resume being sent); subject "{role} at {company} | {story}".
